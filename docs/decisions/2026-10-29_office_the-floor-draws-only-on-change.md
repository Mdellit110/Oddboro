- [office] **Decision (2026-10-29): the floor draws only when something on screen changed, draws only the 16-tile blocks on screen, and steps its ambient animations (water, reeds, machines, dust) on one 12 a second clock.** A still office drew every sprite 60 times a second for nothing; drawing on change cut its CPU about fivefold, and culling by block keeps a draw's cost tied to the screen as offices grow.
