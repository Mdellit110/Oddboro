- [office] **Decision (2026-11-13): a phone the owner turns Buzz me on for gets an empty Web Push when something new lands on the desk, and nothing else.** The push goes through the phone's own push service (Apple's, Google's, Mozilla's or Microsoft's), signed with the office's own key, and carries no words at all: the phone shows a fixed "Something is waiting on your desk." and a tap opens `/desk`. So no third party holds the owner's data and no extra server or app is needed, unlike ntfy. The owner turns it on per phone, on that phone (hard rule 4), and the key and the phones' addresses stay in the system's secret store. Code in `apps/office/src/lib/server/push.ts`, `src/lib/ui/phone-buzz.ts` and `static/desk-push.js`; the setup in `docs/remote-desk.md`.
