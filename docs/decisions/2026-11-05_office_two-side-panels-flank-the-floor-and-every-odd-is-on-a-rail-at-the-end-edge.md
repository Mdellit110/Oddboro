Every Odd stays one click away on the rail, under a ⌘K button, with its count of work in progress and a ring when it waits on the owner; hovering the rail, moving into it with the keyboard, or ⌘K widens the rail itself to show each Odd's name and role with a search, rather than opening a second list. Picking an Odd slides its panel in from the screen's edge and the rail off it (200 ms, none under reduced motion); ⌘K brings the rail back over the panel, and closing or folding the panel brings it back for good. The camera follows the Odd whose panel is open, centred between the panels, until the owner drags; otherwise it stays where the owner drags it (2026-10-31). This supersedes the 2026-10-30 ruling, whose Odds list was a window in the office bar and whose end panel collapsed to a rail of its own.
