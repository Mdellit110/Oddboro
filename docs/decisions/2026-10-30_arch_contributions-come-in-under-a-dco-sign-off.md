- [arch] **Decision (2026-10-30): outside contributions come in under the Developer Certificate of Origin: every commit in a pull request carries a `Signed-off-by:` line from `git commit -s`, and nobody signs a contributor license agreement.** Apache-2.0 already brings contributions in under the same license and lets New Altitude, Inc. use them in closed work, so a CLA would add friction and nothing New Altitude needs. How to sign off is in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
