- [site] **Decision (2026-11-03): each release on /releases/ may carry a looping GIF, drawn once by night and once by day to match the site's theme, and a visitor who asks for reduced motion sees a still of its last frame instead.** The art is the fun part of a release, and a `<picture>` that swaps to a still under `prefers-reduced-motion` meets the accessibility bar with no script. A GIF cannot pause off screen or in a hidden tab, so this is an exception to root rule 13 and to the six-loops ruling, for this page only. Like every other piece of the site's art, a release's art follows the theme, so `art.ts` renders each one in both lights. The GIFs come from `apps/site/scripts/release-art.ts`, the list from `apps/site/src/lib/releases.ts`.
