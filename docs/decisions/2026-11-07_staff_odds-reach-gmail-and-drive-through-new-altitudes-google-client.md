- [staff] **Decision (2026-11-07): Odds reach Gmail and Google Drive through tools the office serves itself, signed in with New Altitude's own Google Desktop client, on every system; New Altitude provides only the sign-in and never receives a token, a message or a file.** No owner makes a Google client, and the token never leaves the office process: the Gmail and Drive tools run inside the office (`builtin` connectors, `apps/office/src/lib/server/google/`) behind the gateway, so a run gets only the gateway's token. Each connector signs in on its own: Gmail with `gmail.modify` (search, read, labels, trash and spam, drafts, send a draft; never permanent delete) and `gmail.settings.basic` (filters), Drive with `drive` (Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides; no delete or trash). A sign-in that lacks a scope signs in afresh. Read only reaches the read tools, and everything else needs write: at Act it goes, and at Draft each call waits for the owner's yes. Replies and filters an Odd proposes wait on the desk, and the office sends a reply or creates a filter only on the owner's click; a filter only files mail, never deletes or forwards. Revoking the last grant cancels the sign-in at Google before the office forgets it. The client's id is in `oauth.ts`; its secret, which Google says a desktop app cannot keep, comes from the build (`ODDBORO_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) and never sits in the repo. Google's hosted MCP servers were measured on 2026-11-07 and refuse tool calls outside its Workspace Developer Preview, so the office's tools take Google's tool names, and moving to Google's servers later is a connector change. The privacy policy says what goes where, including that what an Odd reads goes to the AI service it runs on, and that Windows and Linux keep sign-ins in a shared store. Supersedes the 2026-11-02 ruling that nothing Google ships until New Altitude has its own client. `markBaseline()` in `packages/ledger/src/upgrade.ts` still takes the old connector's rows out of the one install that had them.
