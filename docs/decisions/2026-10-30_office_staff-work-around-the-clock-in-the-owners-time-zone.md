- [office] **Decision (2026-10-30): the office keeps the owner's time zone, which setup asks for and writes to `.env` as `OFFICE_TIME_ZONE` (this Mac's own zone until set). Staff work around the clock, and jobs run on one timer set for the soonest due job, under a login service that keeps the Mac awake on power.** The office is where its owner is, not where the screen is, and agents do not sleep. One timer is the least work that keeps a schedule. Each run records whether its schedule or the owner started it, and a manual run never moves the schedule. Code in `packages/ledger/src/time.ts`, `apps/office/src/lib/server/jobs.ts` and `apps/office/scripts/office-service.sh`.
