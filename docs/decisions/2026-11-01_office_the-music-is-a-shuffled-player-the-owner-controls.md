- [office] **Decision (2026-11-01): the office's music is a player over 25 tracks in a random order, and the owner decides whether it plays.** The first visit opens on "Good Morning, Neighbor". After that the music plays on a visit only if the owner left it on. The owner can love a track (it comes up twice as often), pass on one (it stops coming up), repeat one, and skip back or ahead. Every choice lives in that viewer's browser. The files carry only a title tag; every other tag is stripped. LICENSE-ASSETS keeps the whole audio folder out of the Apache license. Code in `apps/office/src/lib/ui/music.svelte.ts`, `tracks.ts` and `FloorControls.svelte`.
