- [deploy] **Decision (2026-11-02): updates are required, checked on launch and every 4 hours, installed in the owner's window after the Odds are held; releases are drafted on every merge to main and published by hand.** The app installs a newer version before its server starts, and while it runs it downloads one in the background and installs it at the start of the owner's window (02:00 to 04:00 office time unless they change it in Setup), or at once when they press Install now. From 15 minutes before, the job clock and follow-ups start nothing new, and the app waits for running work, at most 20 minutes. Every merge to `main` builds the next patch version as a draft release on New-Altitude/Oddboro, and the app sees it only once the owner publishes it. One version in use means we never support an old one. The detail is in `apps/desktop/CLAUDE.md` (the shell and the release) and `apps/office/CLAUDE.md` (the window and the hold).
