- [office] **Decision (2026-11-14): Talk on a phone call opens the Type box, so the phone keyboard's own mic does the dictation. The page uses no browser speech recognition.** The owner chose it. A home-screen web app on an iPhone often refuses the browser's speech recognition, and Chrome on Android sends the audio to Google, a call out of the office the outbound check would have to declare. The keyboard's mic works on both phones today and sends nothing new from the office. The button says Talk, not Hold to talk, since a tap does it.
