- [art] **Decision (2026-10-31): the office furnishes a room from what each prop says about itself, in its tags, frames and spots (the floor it needs open, whether it looks right against any wall or has a side view, which way its user faces), and from what the building says about each wall (inside or outside); it never places by footprint alone.** Placing by footprint put desks in front of shelves, a couch against the TV, a front-only cabinet on a side wall and sun through inside walls, and the same gaps would break any new theme. A rule that lives with the art serves every room, every theme and every piece the office draws. Rules in `packages/sprites/FORMAT.md`, code in `packages/world/src/generate.ts`.
